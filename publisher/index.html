Why this property publication exists

Property tax planning starts with the investment and how it is used. Acquisition records, service dates, rental activity, owner participation and depreciation all affect the return. A large deduction needs a separate analysis of whether and when the owner can actually use a resulting loss.

Begin with the cost-segregation guide and the placed-in-service evidence checklist. Then read the participation and loss-limit resources. Before a sale, revisit depreciation and the exit model so a first-year deduction is evaluated alongside later consequences.

What to bring to a real estate planning discussion

  • Closing statements, purchase allocations and improvement invoices.
  • Existing depreciation schedules and any cost-segregation reports.
  • Rental calendars, personal-use records and participation evidence.
  • Recent returns, income forecasts and expected holding period.
